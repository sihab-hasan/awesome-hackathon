# Time-Boxed Sprint

Divide the event into milestone blocks rather than assigning detailed estimates.

1. Problem, rules, architecture, and repository.
2. Working vertical slice.
3. Required integrations and seeded data.
4. Reliability, security, and design pass.
5. Pitch, recording, submission, and contingency time.

Reserve at least the final 20% of the event for integration, testing, rehearsal, and submission.
