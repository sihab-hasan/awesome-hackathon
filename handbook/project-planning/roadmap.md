# Post-Hackathon Roadmap

Separate what was demonstrated from what would be required for real users.

- Validation: interviews, evidence, market or operational research.
- Product: missing workflows, accessibility, onboarding, support.
- Engineering: persistence, scaling, observability, testing, security.
- Governance: privacy, compliance, data retention, content policy.
- Delivery: ownership, budget, milestones, and success metrics.
