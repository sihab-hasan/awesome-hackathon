# Minimum Demonstrable Product

A hackathon MVP is the smallest end-to-end experience that proves the idea.

## Definition template

> For **[user]** who struggles with **[problem]**, the product allows them to **[core action]** and receive **[observable outcome]**. The demo proves this by **[evidence]**.

## Scope gate

A feature belongs in the MVP only when removing it makes the core claim impossible to demonstrate.
