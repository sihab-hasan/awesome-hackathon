# Choosing a Team

The ideal team is small enough to communicate continuously and broad enough to cover product, implementation, and presentation.

## Practical roles

- **Product owner:** problem definition, scope, rules, and final submission.
- **Builder:** core implementation and integration.
- **Experience owner:** interface, usability, visual consistency, and accessibility.
- **Story owner:** research, pitch, demo script, evidence, and video.

One person may own multiple roles. Every critical task needs exactly one directly responsible owner.

## Team agreement

Before building, agree on communication channel, repository access, branch strategy, decision process, working hours, attribution, prize handling, and what happens if a member leaves.
