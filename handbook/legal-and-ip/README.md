# Legal, Licensing, and Intellectual Property

Review the event's eligibility, ownership, prior-work, open-source, sponsor, publicity, dataset, and submission terms before building.

## Repository hygiene

- Record third-party libraries, assets, models, datasets, and APIs.
- Preserve required copyright and attribution notices.
- Confirm that the chosen license is compatible with every redistributed dependency and asset.
- Do not publish employer, university, customer, or teammate confidential information.
- Disclose meaningful pre-existing work when event rules require it.

## Team agreement

Before submission, agree on ownership, credits, repository access, prize handling, and what happens after the event. This guide is general information, not legal advice.
