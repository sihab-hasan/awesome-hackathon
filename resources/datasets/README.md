# Datasets Resources

Curated resources for hackathon teams. Last catalog generation: **2026-08-07**.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Data.gov](https://data.gov/) | Civic and public-data projects | Free | Official | Low |
| [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) | Data science prototypes | Free | Official | Low |
| [WHO Data](https://data.who.int/) | Health and public-policy projects | Free | Official | Low |
| [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) | Social-impact projects | Free | Official | Low |

## Selection guidance

Prefer the smallest tool the team already understands. Confirm current pricing, quotas, regional availability, event eligibility, and data-handling terms before committing the demo to a provider.
