# Hardware and IoT Pack

A focused subset of the canonical catalog for this delivery scenario.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Adafruit Learning System](https://learn.adafruit.com/) | Hardware learning and examples | Free | Official | Low |
| [Arduino Documentation](https://docs.arduino.cc/) | Microcontroller prototypes | Open source hardware and free tools | Official | Low |
| [Edge Impulse](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/) | TinyML prototypes | Free tier and paid | Official | Low |
| [PlatformIO](https://docs.platformio.org/) | Reproducible embedded projects | Open source | Official | Low |
| [Raspberry Pi Documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/) | Single-board computer projects | Free documentation; hardware required | Official | Low |
| [Wokwi](https://docs.wokwi.com/) | Hardware demos without every physical part | Free and paid options | Official | Low |
| [Apache Kafka](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/) | High-throughput event streaming when the team already knows Kafka | Open source | Official | Medium |
| [CloudAMQP](https://www.cloudamqp.com/docs/) | Hosted RabbitMQ queues with operational dashboards | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [NATS](https://docs.nats.io/) | Fast messaging and event-driven system prototypes | Open source | Official | Medium |
| [Upstash QStash](https://upstash.com/docs/qstash/overall/getstarted) | Background jobs without operating a message broker | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Ably](https://ably.com/docs/) | Reliable hosted realtime features with minimal infrastructure work | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Pusher](https://pusher.com/docs/) | Quick hosted realtime channels and notifications | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Socket.IO](https://socket.io/docs/v4/) | Realtime prototypes controlled by a JavaScript team | Open source | Official | Medium |
| [Supabase Realtime](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime) | Realtime features in projects already using Supabase | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/) | Broad infrastructure needs | Free tier and paid | Official | Medium |
| [GitHub Codespaces](https://docs.github.com/en/codespaces) | Consistent team setup | Quota based | Official | Medium |
| [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/docs) | Cloud and AI prototypes | Free credits and paid | Official | Medium |
| [Microsoft Azure](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/) | Microsoft ecosystem projects | Free credits and paid | Official | Medium |
| [Apache ECharts](https://echarts.apache.org/en/option.html) | Dashboards and complex charts | Open source | Official | Low |
| [D3.js](https://d3js.org/getting-started) | Custom interactive charts | Open source | Official | Low |

Use this pack as a decision aid, not as a requirement to adopt every listed tool.
