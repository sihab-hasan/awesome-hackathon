# Design Resources

Curated resources for hackathon teams. Last catalog generation: **2026-08-07**.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) | Pitch decks and visual assets | Free tier and paid | Official | Low |
| [Excalidraw](https://docs.excalidraw.com/) | Fast architecture diagrams | Open source | Official | Low |
| [Figma](https://help.figma.com/) | UI design and clickable prototypes | Free tier and paid | Official | Low |
| [Penpot](https://help.penpot.app/) | Open design workflows | Open source and hosted | Official | Low |

## Selection guidance

Prefer the smallest tool the team already understands. Confirm current pricing, quotas, regional availability, event eligibility, and data-handling terms before committing the demo to a provider.
