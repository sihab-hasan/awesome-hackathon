# Apis Resources

Curated resources for hackathon teams. Last catalog generation: **2026-08-07**.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [JSONPlaceholder](https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com/) | Frontend prototypes | Free | Primary | Low |
| [Mockaroo](https://www.mockaroo.com/) | Demo datasets | Free tier and paid | Primary | Low |
| [Public APIs](https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis) | Finding data and service APIs | Varies | Community | Low |
| [RapidAPI Hub](https://rapidapi.com/hub) | Quick third-party integrations | Free and paid APIs | Official | Low |

## Selection guidance

Prefer the smallest tool the team already understands. Confirm current pricing, quotas, regional availability, event eligibility, and data-handling terms before committing the demo to a provider.
