# Presentation Resources

Curated resources for hackathon teams. Last catalog generation: **2026-08-07**.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Google Slides](https://workspace.google.com/products/slides/) | Team pitch decks | Free and paid | Official | Low |
| [Loom](https://www.loom.com/) | Asynchronous demo videos | Free tier and paid | Official | Low |
| [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) | Backup demo recordings | Open source | Official | Low |

## Selection guidance

Prefer the smallest tool the team already understands. Confirm current pricing, quotas, regional availability, event eligibility, and data-handling terms before committing the demo to a provider.
