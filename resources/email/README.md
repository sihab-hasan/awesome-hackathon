# Email Resources

Curated resources for hackathon teams. Last catalog generation: **2026-08-07**.

| Resource | Best for | Cost | Source | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Mailgun](https://documentation.mailgun.com/docs/mailgun/) | Email routing, validation, and API-driven delivery | Trial and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Postmark](https://postmarkapp.com/developer/) | Delivery-focused transactional email prototypes | Trial and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Resend](https://resend.com/docs/introduction) | Sending transactional email from modern web applications | Free tier and paid plans | Official | Medium |
| [Twilio SendGrid](https://www.twilio.com/docs/sendgrid) | Teams needing a broad email API and template ecosystem | Free trial and paid plans | Official | Medium |

## Selection guidance

Prefer the smallest tool the team already understands. Confirm current pricing, quotas, regional availability, event eligibility, and data-handling terms before committing the demo to a provider.
