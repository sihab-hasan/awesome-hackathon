# Conflicts and Recusal

## Conflicts to disclose

Disclose employment, investment, mentorship, family or close personal relationships, prior project collaboration, direct competition, sponsor obligations, public advocacy, or any interest that could reasonably affect impartiality.

## Recusal procedure

Report the conflict before judging begins when possible. The judging lead decides reassignment using the published policy. A recused judge must not score, lobby, or access restricted deliberation for that project.

## Incidental familiarity

Prior awareness of a public project is not automatically disqualifying, but undisclosed pre-existing relationships or privileged knowledge require review.

## Records

Record the project, conflict category, decision, replacement judge, and effect on score aggregation. Share only the minimum information necessary to preserve fairness and privacy.
