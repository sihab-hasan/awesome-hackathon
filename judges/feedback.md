# Constructive Judge Feedback

## Evidence-based format

A useful note contains:

1. The demonstrated strength or gap
2. The evidence observed
3. The user or technical consequence
4. One realistic next validation step

## Good feedback

Focus on problem evidence, working behavior, architecture decisions, data handling, failure modes, accessibility, safety, and demo clarity. Distinguish missing evidence from incorrect implementation.

## Avoid

Do not provide personal judgments, vague praise, undisclosed commercial pitches, requests for free work, promises about prizes or investment, or advice that contradicts event rules.

## Consistency

Apply similar depth to every team. Feedback is not a substitute for scoring evidence and should not reveal confidential deliberation or another team’s work.
