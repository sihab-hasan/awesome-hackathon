# Judge Calibration

## Goal

Calibration reduces variance without forcing identical opinions. Judges should agree on evidence standards, scoring anchors, conflicts, and procedure before evaluating live projects.

## Process

1. Review the published rubric and tie-break order.
2. Score at least one representative sample independently.
3. Compare evidence and reasoning, not only totals.
4. Resolve interpretation of incomplete demos, simulated data, pre-existing work, AI assistance, accessibility accommodations, and sponsor requirements.
5. Record unresolved policy questions and obtain an organizer ruling visible to every judge.
6. Confirm timekeeping, note-taking, recusal, and score-submission procedures.

## Variance control

Large score differences should trigger evidence review, not automatic averaging. Judges retain independent judgment but must apply the same published criteria. Do not introduce new criteria during deliberation.
