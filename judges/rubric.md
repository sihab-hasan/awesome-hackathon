# Judging Rubric

Use a 100-point rubric unless event rules specify otherwise.

| Dimension | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Problem and user understanding | 20 | Clear target user, credible need, and evidence that the problem matters |
| Working outcome | 25 | Core workflow functions end to end and produces a useful result |
| Technical judgment | 20 | Appropriate architecture, integration quality, data handling, and failure awareness |
| Innovation and differentiation | 10 | Distinct insight, combination, workflow, or technical approach |
| Responsible design | 10 | Security, privacy, accessibility, safety, and realistic limitations |
| Demo and communication | 10 | Coherent narrative, visible evidence, and reliable demonstration |
| Continuation potential | 5 | Plausible next validation step and maintainable project direction |

## Scoring anchors

A score near zero means the dimension is absent or contradicted by evidence. Half credit means a credible partial implementation. Full credit requires demonstrated, specific, and consistent evidence.

## Tie-break order

1. Working outcome
2. Problem and user understanding
3. Responsible design
4. Technical judgment
5. Independent judge review

Sponsor prizes may use additional criteria, but they must not alter general-track scoring unless announced in advance.
