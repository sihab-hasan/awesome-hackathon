# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, accessible, and harassment-free community for contributors and users regardless of experience, disability, age, body size, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical background.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Respect privacy, attribution, consent, and personal boundaries.
- Disclose relevant affiliations and conflicts.
- Critique content and evidence rather than people.
- Use accessible language and accommodate different communication needs.
- Accept moderation and review decisions without retaliation or pressure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, stalking, doxxing, impersonation, unwanted private contact, deliberate misinformation, plagiarism, credential exposure, malicious links, spam, affiliate promotion, or pressure for access, ranking, prizes, employment, or investment is prohibited.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through repository-owner contact methods or other published private channels. Do not post sensitive incident details in an issue or discussion. Include only the information needed to understand immediate risk and requested support.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request correction, edit or remove content, hide or lock interactions, restrict participation, or refer matters to platform or legal authorities when necessary for safety. Response information is shared only with people who need it to act.

## Scope

This code applies to repository spaces, project communications, and representation of the community in related public or private interactions.
