# Virtual and Hybrid Events

## Platform design

Choose platforms that support low-bandwidth participation, captions, keyboard navigation, recording consent, moderated rooms, private support, and reliable access from participant regions. Do not require unnecessary camera use.

## Fairness

Remote and in-person teams must receive equivalent access to rules, mentors, workshops, submission support, judging time, and service-status information. Record sessions or provide written alternatives when time zones or connectivity create barriers.

## Identity and safety

Define account naming, moderation, private reporting, recording, screenshots, direct messaging, minors, impersonation, and removal procedures. Limit organizer privileges and review platform audit capabilities before launch.

## Technical rehearsal

Test registration, room permissions, screen sharing, captions, recording, mentor queue, judge rooms, submission links, live stream, and emergency announcements using participant and judge accounts.

## Continuity

Provide alternate meeting links, dial-in or text support, local recording fallback, downloadable schedules, and an outage decision tree. State how deadlines and judging change when a platform-wide failure occurs.
