# Post-Event Operations

## Immediate close

Secure submission exports, scores, incident records, consent records, financial evidence, and prize eligibility documents. Revoke temporary credentials and organizer access that is no longer required.

## Results and prizes

Verify calculations, conflicts, eligibility, challenge requirements, names, and prize terms before publication. Communicate delays directly. Record prize delivery, tax or identity requirements, failed transfers, and escalation.

## Participant follow-up

Share results, feedback routes, project continuation resources, recordings or materials, data-retention information, and a correction process. Do not publish participant or project details beyond consent and event terms.

## Retrospective

Review participation, completion, support volume, service failures, safety incidents, accessibility, judging variance, sponsor outcomes, budget, submission friction, and project continuation. Assign an owner and target release for each approved improvement.

## Archive

Preserve public rules, challenge briefs, schedules, official results, project links, aggregate statistics, and release notes. Delete or restrict personal and sensitive operational data according to the published retention policy.
