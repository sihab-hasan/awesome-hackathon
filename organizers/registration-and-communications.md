# Registration and Communications

## Registration data

Collect only data required for eligibility, access, safety, accommodations, communications, team formation, and prize administration. Explain purpose, retention, access, correction, and deletion routes. Keep optional demographic questions separate from eligibility decisions.

## Participant communications

Use one canonical event page and one primary announcement channel. Publish dates with timezone, eligibility, team size, rules, code of conduct, IP terms, schedule, venue or platform access, accessibility support, challenge briefs, submission requirements, judging, prizes, and support contacts.

## Communication cadence

Send confirmation, preparation guidance, final logistics, opening instructions, deadline reminders, incident or service updates, results, prize verification, feedback, and retention notices. Avoid unnecessary promotional messages and do not silently change material rules.

## Support model

Separate public questions, private eligibility questions, accessibility requests, safety reports, and technical incidents. Define response owners, expected response windows, escalation, and after-hours coverage.

## Failure fallback

Maintain downloadable rules, an alternate announcement channel, an offline participant list with restricted access, a manual check-in process, and a documented extension policy for platform-wide failures.
