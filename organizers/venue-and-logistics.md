# Venue and Logistics

## Site readiness

Confirm capacity, accessible routes, quiet rooms, toilets, prayer or reflection space where appropriate, ventilation, power, fire safety, first aid, food handling, waste management, transport, overnight policy, and emergency evacuation.

## Technical readiness

Survey network capacity, wireless coverage, power distribution, display adapters, audio, streaming, badge printing, check-in fallback, isolated hardware zones, and service credentials. Keep a wired organizer connection and offline copies of schedules, contacts, rules, and submissions procedures.

## Operations map

Publish the location of check-in, work areas, help desk, mentor queue, judging rooms, food, quiet space, accessibility support, first aid, emergency exits, and restricted areas. Assign an operational owner and backup for each zone.

## Hardware controls

Define rules for soldering, batteries, mains voltage, lasers, drones, moving machinery, radio equipment, chemicals, and biological materials. Require supervision and designated work areas for higher-risk activities.

## Close-down

Plan participant departure, equipment return, lost property, data export, waste removal, venue inspection, financial sign-off, and incident handoff before the event begins.
