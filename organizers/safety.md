# Safety and Conduct Operations

## Required controls

Publish the code of conduct, confidential reporting routes, response owners, emergency contacts, accessibility support, photography policy, minor-participant rules, venue safety procedures, and online moderation policy before registration opens.

## Incident intake

Accept reports through more than one channel. Record the time, reporter preference, immediate safety risk, involved parties, available evidence, and requested support. Restrict access to the smallest trained response group.

## Response sequence

1. Protect immediate safety.
2. Separate involved parties when appropriate.
3. Preserve evidence without broad redistribution.
4. Apply the published process consistently.
5. Communicate only what each party needs to know.
6. Document actions and follow-up.

## Technical safety

Prohibit credential sharing, malicious scanning, unauthorized access, harmful datasets, non-consensual surveillance, and unsafe hardware operation. Provide an isolated network or sandbox for security challenges.

## Accessibility and wellbeing

Provide quiet space, breaks, water, food labeling, accessible routes, captioning or interpretation when possible, and a clear way to request accommodations. Do not reward dangerous sleep deprivation.

## Aftercare

Offer follow-up contact, preserve records according to policy, review systemic failures, and update procedures without exposing reporters or affected participants.
