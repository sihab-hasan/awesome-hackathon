## Operational contacts

Record the event lead, safety lead, technical lead, venue or platform lead, submission lead, judging lead, communications lead, and backup contacts.

## Decision points

Document who may change the schedule, extend a deadline, pause the event, remove a participant, switch platforms, or delay results. Record each decision and announcement time.
