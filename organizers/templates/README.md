# Organizer Templates

These templates are neutral operational records. Adapt them to local law, venue requirements, safeguarding rules, and organizational policy.

- [Challenge brief](challenge-brief.md)
- [Run of show](run-of-show.md)
- [Incident record](incident-record.md)
- [Judge calibration record](judge-calibration.md)
- [Mentor handoff](mentor-handoff.md)
- [Submission specification](submission-specification.md)

Do not place secrets, unnecessary personal information, medical details, or unverified allegations in shared templates. Restrict sensitive records to authorized responders.
