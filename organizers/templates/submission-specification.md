# Submission Specification Template

## Deadline

Record the exact date, time, timezone, late policy, extension authority, and platform-outage procedure.

## Required fields

- Team and eligibility confirmation
- Problem and target user
- Working outcome
- Repository and commit reference
- Demo or video
- Setup instructions
- Architecture summary
- External services and datasets
- Pre-existing work disclosure
- Team contributions
- License
- Security, privacy, accessibility, and safety notes
- Known limitations
- Sponsor-challenge evidence where applicable

## Validation and retention

Define automated checks, human exceptions, immutable export, correction policy, judge access, retention period, deletion route, and results publication fields.
