# Incident Record Template

## Intake

- Record ID:
- Received time and channel:
- Response owner:
- Immediate safety risk:
- Reporter communication preference:

## Minimum necessary facts

Record what happened, when, where, involved event roles, available evidence, requested support, and potential ongoing risk. Avoid unnecessary sensitive detail.

## Actions

Record protective action, access restriction, evidence preservation, communication, policy basis, escalation, and review time.

## Closure

Record outcome, follow-up owner, retention classification, systemic improvement, and whether affected people received the permitted closure communication.
