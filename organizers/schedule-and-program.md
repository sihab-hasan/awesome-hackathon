# Schedule and Program

## Program design

Protect uninterrupted build time. Use workshops only when they reduce predictable blockers. Schedule opening, rules briefing, challenge clarification, team formation, mentor rounds, service-status updates, submission rehearsal, final deadline, judging, results, and close-down.

## Time control

Every program item needs an owner, start and end time, location or link, required audience, accessibility support, recording decision, and fallback. Publish one canonical schedule and timestamp changes.

## Suggested operational checkpoints

- Opening: rules, conduct, support, deadlines, judging, and incident routes
- Early build: team and environment check
- Midpoint: scope, risk, and dependency review
- Pre-submission: repository, video, deployment, and form rehearsal
- Deadline: incident log and extension decision
- Judging: calibration, conflicts, timekeeping, score completeness
- Close: prize verification, feedback, retention, and continued support

## Fatigue management

Do not design schedules that reward unsafe sleep deprivation. Provide breaks, food windows, quiet periods, and a clear overnight policy.
