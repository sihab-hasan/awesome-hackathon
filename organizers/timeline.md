# Organizer Timeline

## 12–16 weeks before

Confirm event purpose, accountable owner, budget authority, legal review, insurance, venue or virtual platform, accessibility lead, safety lead, privacy model, eligibility, intellectual-property terms, prize feasibility, and cancellation criteria.

## 8–12 weeks before

Secure sponsors and vendors, publish initial rules, open registration, draft challenges, define judging, establish communications, recruit mentors and judges, and plan data retention and incident response.

## 4–8 weeks before

Finalize challenges, schedule, venue map, technical services, participant support, accessibility arrangements, submission specification, judge calibration material, mentor rota, hardware controls, and contingency plans.

## 1–4 weeks before

Test the complete participant and judge journey. Rehearse check-in, streaming, captions, mentor queue, support, submission, scoring, result calculation, outage communication, credential revocation, and emergency response.

## Event week

Freeze material terms, brief all operators, confirm funding and prizes, issue credentials, publish final logistics, run platform and venue checks, activate incident channels, and maintain an operational decision log.

## During the event

Monitor safety, accessibility, support volume, service status, mentor fairness, schedule, submission readiness, and participant communications. Apply extensions or rule clarifications consistently and visibly.

## Within 72 hours after

Verify results and prizes, secure evidence, revoke temporary access, publish permitted outcomes, communicate delays, close immediate incidents, and send participant follow-up.

## Within 2–4 weeks after

Complete financial reconciliation, retention actions, sponsor reporting, accessibility review, incident follow-up, project archive, feedback analysis, and a retrospective with assigned improvements.
