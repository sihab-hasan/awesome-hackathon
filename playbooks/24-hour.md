# 24-Hour Hackathon Playbook

## Hours 0–2

Rules, team agreement, problem statement, judging matrix, repository, deployment placeholder.

## Hours 2–7

Build the smallest vertical slice with seeded data.

## Hours 7–13

Complete required integration, authorization, and main interface.

## Hours 13–17

Reliability, validation, security, and responsive-design pass.

## Hours 17–21

Pitch, screenshots, recording, README, and submission draft.

## Hours 21–24

Freeze scope, rehearse, test clean deployment, submit early.

## Exit condition

The core claim is demonstrable, limitations are explicit, and the team has a tested fallback.
