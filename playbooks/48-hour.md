# 48-Hour Hackathon Playbook

## Hours 0–4

Problem evidence, scope, architecture, roles, repository, deployment.

## Hours 4–16

Vertical slice and primary integration.

## Hours 16–28

Complete core workflow and product experience.

## Hours 28–36

Testing, accessibility, security, observability, and fallback paths.

## Hours 36–44

Pitch, demo recording, documentation, and submission.

## Hours 44–48

Feature freeze, rehearsals, contingency, final verification.

## Exit condition

The core claim is demonstrable, limitations are explicit, and the team has a tested fallback.
