# Project Showcase

The showcase records completed hackathon projects without implying endorsement, production readiness, or award status.

## Submission fields

Include the event, problem, target user, team, repository, live demo or video, license, technology summary, accessibility and safety notes, and a concise update describing what continued after the event.

## Acceptance rules

The project must have a working public artifact, clear attribution, and enough documentation for a reader to understand the demonstrated outcome. Projects containing exposed secrets, harmful content, copied work, misleading claims, or inaccessible evidence are rejected.

## Maintenance

Showcase links are reviewed periodically. Entries may be archived when the project disappears or the repository no longer explains what was built.
