# Sponsorship Policy

Resources are not ranked or included in exchange for payment, gifts, credits, or affiliate revenue.

## Disclosure

Contributors and maintainers must disclose employment, investment, referral, ambassador, grant, or sponsorship relationships relevant to a proposed resource.

## Sponsor resources

Sponsor tools are evaluated under the same curation standard as every other entry. Event-specific credits, quotas, and eligibility must be verified from official terms.

## Recognition

Financial or infrastructure support for the repository may be acknowledged separately without editorial control over catalog inclusion.
