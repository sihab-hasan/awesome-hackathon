# Contributors

Contributors improve catalog data, guides, blueprints, validation tools, event operations, translations, and accessibility.

## Attribution

Git history and release notes are the authoritative contribution record. Significant non-code contributions may also be credited in acknowledgements with the contributor’s consent.

## Expectations

Contributions must follow the code of conduct, disclose affiliations, avoid promotional language, preserve source attribution, and pass repository validation.

## Maintainer path

Consistent contributors may be invited to review a category or operational area. Maintainer access follows least privilege and may be removed after prolonged inactivity or policy violations.
