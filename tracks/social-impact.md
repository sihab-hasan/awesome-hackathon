# Social Impact Track

## Outcome

A solution developed with affected users or credible domain evidence, not assumptions.

## Delivery principles

- Avoid savior framing.
- Minimize sensitive data.
- Design for access constraints.
- Explain unintended harm.
- Measure a realistic outcome.

## Required evidence

Show the user problem, one end-to-end workflow, architecture boundary, test evidence, failure fallback, and the next validation step.

## Common failure

Teams lose time by adding technologies before proving the core interaction. Freeze the stack once the first vertical slice works.
